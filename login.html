Welcome to Vehicle Speed Estimation

Estimate vehicle speed in real time using camera-based computer vision for intelligent traffic monitoring and smart city solutions.

This system detects and tracks vehicles from traffic video footage and calculates their speed by analyzing movement across consecutive frames.

The application supports secure user access, video upload, and live visualization of processed results.

  • Camera-based vehicle detection and tracking
  • Accurate real-time speed estimation
  • Smart traffic monitoring and enforcement
